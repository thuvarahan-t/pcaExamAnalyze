How the figures are calculated

All marks use the current accepted answer key. Rank counts only submitted papers. Equal marks share the same rank; the next rank skips tied places (for example 1, 2, 2, 4). The denominator in your rank is the total number of submitted papers.

Question-level correct percentages include unanswered papers in their total. A free mark is awarded regardless of the selected answer and is not treated as evidence of subject knowledge. The pie chart shows free marks separately when present.
