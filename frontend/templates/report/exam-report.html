Appendix: Definitions & Method

How the figures in this report are calculated.

MarksOne mark per correct answer; no negative marking. Questions the teacher marked as free mark count as correct for every student. Marks are recalculated from the current answer key when this report is generated.
PercentageMarks divided by the number of questions, times 100.
Pass rateShare of students scoring 50% or more.
GradesA: 75% and above. B: 65-74%. C: 50-64%. S: 35-49%. F: below 35%.
Mean, median, std devMean is the average percentage. Median is the middle student. Standard deviation shows how spread out the scores are (population formula).
Correct %Students who answered the question correctly divided by all students who submitted the paper (blank answers count as not correct).
DifficultyEasy: 70% or more answered correctly. Moderate: 40-69%. Difficult: below 40%.
Discrimination indexCorrect share of the top 27% of students (by total marks) minus that of the bottom 27%. 0.40 and above is excellent, 0.30-0.39 good, 0.20-0.29 fair, below 0.20 poor, and a negative value means weaker students did better than stronger ones - check the answer key. It needs at least 10 submissions.
Most chosen wrongThe wrong option picked by the most students - useful for spotting a common misconception.
Average timeMean time students spent viewing the question (question-by-question exams only), compared with the expected time set by the teacher.
Syllabus unitsQuestions are grouped by the syllabus units tagged in the Question Editor; the unit score is the average Correct % of its questions.
DistrictThe district each student entered in their details form.

Generated by the PCA MCQ Examination System on date.
